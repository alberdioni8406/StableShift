How it works

You receive BCH → convert to a stablecoin → hold $1-pegged value, no CEX required.

STEP 01

Receive BCH

Accept BCH as payment or receive it in any CashTokens-compatible wallet like Paytaca, Cashonize, or Electron Cash.

STEP 02

Choose your stablecoin

Pick MUSD (Moria, 150% min collateral) or PUSD (ParyonUSD, 110% min collateral). Both are BCH-backed and decentralized.

STEP 03

Open a loan or swap

Deposit BCH as collateral on Moria or ParyonUSD to mint stablecoins — or swap directly on Cauldron DEX for instant conversion.

STEP 04

Hold stable, reclaim BCH

Your BCH stays locked as collateral. Repay the stablecoin at any time to get your BCH back — at the new price.
